Design points worth keeping:

- **Work in `compute`, JavaScript values in `resolve`** (Andrew's napi skill). A reader whose result carries bytes
  has two forms: `ImageRead`, `MediaRead`, `DocumentRead` hold `Vec<u8>` and are built on the worker thread;
  `images_js`, `media_js`, `document_js` make the `Buffer`s back on the JavaScript thread. The `tasks!` macro has a
  short arm (one type, one reader) and a long arm (read type, JavaScript type, reader, converter). The read types
  are `pub` inside the private `types` module because they are a `Task::Output` of a `pub` task.
- The `Shape` union is flat: every variant repeats the seven common fields (`frame`, `stacking`, `shapeName`,
  `altText`, `placeholder`, `uri`, `dest`). In Rust a `Common` struct and `common_of(info)` fill them.
- An annotation's `rect` is `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` in the page frame of `layout()` (y up), as pdfdown gives it. A
  shape's or a picture's `frame` is `{ x, y, width, height, rotation }` from the slide's top-left corner.
- `slides()[n].page` is the slide number: `page` everywhere, never `number`.
- Free functions take a buffer only and read every slide; the option is on the class.
