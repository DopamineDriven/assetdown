### A real fix found on the way: how numbered lists are counted

`crates/pptxdown/src/body.rs`, `read_paragraphs`. The counter was keyed by scheme alone, so a paragraph stating a
different `startAt` in the middle of a shape went on counting instead of starting there. Now:

- Paragraphs that follow each other on a level with one scheme **and one stated start** are one list, counted
  from that start (`struct Counted { scheme, start, next }`).
- A paragraph that states another scheme or another start begins a new list there.
- A character bullet, or text with no bullet (stated or not), ends the list on its level.
- A paragraph with nothing in it shows no number, takes none, and ends nothing.

Evidence in a public file: the older deck's second slide types step 1 by hand, then numbers five paragraphs that
state starts of 2, 2, 3, 3, 3, two of them empty. The steps read 2, 3, 4. Unit test:
`a_numbered_paragraph_with_nothing_in_it_shows_no_number_and_takes_none` in `body/tests.rs`.

## A lesson about counting XML with grep

A one-line `grep -o '<p:ph[^>]*>'` found no placeholders in the wide deck, because that deck writes its tags
across several lines. Join lines first (`tr '\n' ' '`) before counting elements that carry attributes. Counts of
bare tags such as `<p:pic>` were not affected.
