## 2. In flight: a workbook's total and line items from its formulas

The user said yes to building this in the library (`xlsxdown`, so `xlsdown` gets it through the shared `Workbook`). The work-laptop prototype showed the formula graph beats labels for a workbook's total by a wide margin, and `invoice()` returns no line items today.

**Done, uncommitted, tests passing:**

- `crates/xlsxdown/src/formula.rs`: `pub(crate) fn references(formula) -> Vec<Reference>` (`Reference { sheet: Option<String>, range: CellRange, start, end }`). It finds the cells and ranges a formula refers to on this workbook's sheets, and passes over text, names, functions, numbers, errors, table columns, other workbooks, and runs of sheets. Four tests in `formula/tests.rs`. Uses an `if let` match guard.
- `crates/xlsxdown/src/audit.rs` (new) with `audit/tests.rs` (13 tests): `Workbook::audit() -> Option<Audit>`; `Audit { total: CellAt, value, lines: Vec<AuditLine>, adds_up }`; `AuditLine { at, amount, description, quantity, unit_price }`; `CellAt { sheet, row, col }`. Exported from `lib.rs`.
  - The total: of the formulas nothing reads, on shown sheets, the one that reaches the most cells; then one shown as money (`currency_symbol`, or a format with `.00`); then the lowest.
  - Lines: follow the total down. A formula that only adds cells up (`SUM`, `+`, `-`, a `ROUND` of the whole, `SUBTOTAL(9,` or `(109,`) is a subtotal and is followed; anything else (a typed number, quantity times price, a charge worked out from a subtotal) is a line. What a total takes away is a line below nought. Lines of nought are dropped. Other sheets are followed, hidden ones included.
  - A line that multiplies a total (hours in all times a rate) gives way to the rows that come to the same amount under a sum of their own (`in_its_place`).
  - Quantity and unit price: for a line that is one cell times another, the price is the one shown as money; neither or both shown so, unknown.
  - Description: the row's text to the left, else to the right, else the heading over the column.
  - `adds_up`: the lines come to the total, half a cent to a line. A total that is more than a sum of cells has no lines.
  - `MAX_READS` (4,000,000 cells read between all formulas): past it, `audit()` is `None`.
